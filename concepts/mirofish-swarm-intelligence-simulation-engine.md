# 🐟 MiroFish: Universal Swarm Intelligence & Social Simulation Engine (82k+ Stars)

> **"Rehearse the future in a digital sandbox, and win decisions after countless simulations. Upload your startup pitch, policy, or product idea; MiroFish spawns an entire synthetic society of diverse AI agents who debate, purchase, or reject it on a simulated social platform."**  
> Technical breakdown of **MiroFish** (`666ghj/MiroFish`), the viral open-source swarm intelligence engine that constructs high-fidelity parallel digital worlds to stress-test business ideas and predict collective human behavior.

---

## ⚡ What Is MiroFish?

**Repository:** `https://github.com/666ghj/MiroFish` (82,000+ GitHub Stars)  
**Core Innovation:** Traditional market research relies on static surveys, focus groups, or singular LLM opinions (which suffer from sycophancy). MiroFish utilizes **Agent-Based Modeling (ABM) combined with Generative Swarm Intelligence**:
1. You provide a **seed document** (a startup pitch deck, landing page, new pricing tier, product concept, or airport policy change).
2. The engine generates **hundreds to thousands of diverse, heterogeneous autonomous agents**—each assigned unique demographics, income levels, cognitive biases, past memories, and risk tolerances.
3. The agents are released into a **simulated social feed / forum environment** (mimicking X, Reddit, or consumer marketplaces).
4. Agents read, comment, share, argue, form factions, spread viral sentiment, and make purchase/churn decisions.
5. The engine synthesizes a predictive **Intelligence Assessment Report** detailing real-world adoption probability, failure vectors, and unexpected behavioral emergence.

## 🛠️ Key Engine Features

1. **Heterogeneous Personas:** Agents don't share a single perspective; some are hyper-skeptical budget hawks, early-adopter techies, conservative corporate procurement leads, or easily frustrated consumers.
2. **Dynamic "God-Eye" Injections:** In the middle of an active simulation, you can dynamically inject breaking events (e.g., *"A competitor launches a clone at 50% discount"* or *"A flight gets delayed 4 hours"*) and watch how public sentiment ripples across the population.
3. **Local & Offline Execution:** Can run entirely offline using local models via Ollama + Neo4j / Graphiti (via `MiroFish-Offline`), preserving privacy for proprietary business plans.

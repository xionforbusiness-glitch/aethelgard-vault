# 🇸🇾 Syrian Airlines (السورية للطيران) — Visual Identity, Livery & Staff Dress Code

A comprehensive breakdown of the modern brand architecture, aircraft livery transformation, and crew/ground staff **uniform & dress code standards** for **Syrian Arab Airlines (Syrian Air — `RB / SYR`)**.

---

## 🎨 1. Brand Architecture & Color Palette

The rebrand modernizes the iconic Syrian heritage motifs into a sleek, aerodynamic international aviation aesthetic:

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                 SYRIAN AIR COLOR PALETTE                    │
├─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ 1. Deep Levantine Navy   (#0B1B3D) — Primary Authority      │
│ 2. Damascene Warm Gold   (#C5A059) — Heritage Accent        │
│ 3. Aerodynamic Pearl     (#F4F6F9) — Fuselage & Base White  │
│ 4. Sky Cyan Glow         (#00A3E0) — Wingtip & Digital      │

```

* **The Logo Redesign:** An aerodynamic evolution of the legendary **Syrian winged bird (طائر السورية)**, refined with clean geometric contours for high readability across digital screens, tailfins, and baggage tags.

---

## 👔 2. Staff Dress Code & Uniform Guidelines (كود اللباس والزي الموحد)

As part of the identity overhaul, new uniform guidelines define the visual standard for staff across airport stations and aircraft cabins:

### A. Airport Ground Services & Check-in Counter Staff (موظفو الخدمات الأرضية)
* **Male Agents:**
  * **Suit:** Tailored deep navy two-piece suit with notch lapels.
  * **Shirt:** Crisp, ironed plain white formal shirt (French cuff or button cuff).
  * **Tie:** Silk woven tie in deep navy with subtle Damascene gold pin-striping or micro-pattern.
  * **Accessories:** Silver/Gold metal Syrian Air lapel pin on left chest; polished black formal leather shoes and black socks.
* **Female Agents:**
  * **Outfit:** Tailored navy blazer paired with matching straight-cut formal trousers or knee-length pencil skirt.
  * **Blouse & Scarf:** White structured blouse accented with a lightweight silk neck scarf featuring gold and navy Damascene motifs.
  * **Footwear:** Professional closed-toe black heels (2–4 cm) or flats.

---

### B. Cabin Crew & Flight Attendants (طاقم الضيافة الجوية)
* **Design Philosophy:** Combines contemporary international elegance with authentic Syrian textile heritage (incorporating subtle brocade/damask silk textures).
* **Pursers & Senior Cabin Crew:** Distinguished by gold-trimmed piping along the jacket cuffs and a formal double-breasted vest.
* **Grooming Mandate:** Neat hair styling (buns with hairnets for long hair), minimalist natural makeup, and strict prohibition of visible non-standard jewelry.

---

### C. Flight Deck / Cockpit Crew (طاقم القيادة)
* **Captain (4 Gold Stripes):** Classic midnight navy jacket with 4 gold bullion sleeve braids, gold embroidered winged pilot badge, and gold-embroidered visor peak cap.
* **First Officer (3 Gold Stripes):** Matching uniform with 3 gold sleeve braids.

## 🏢 4. Damascus Airport (`DAM / OSDI`) Counter Touchpoints

1. **Check-in Signage:** High-contrast LED backlights displaying the new golden emblem on midnight navy backgrounds.
2. **Boarding Passes & Bag Tags:** Clean modern layouts with clear baggage zone color coding to expedite boarding at Terminal 1.
3. **VIP & Lounge Presentation:** Warm ambient lighting paired with brass gold architectural metalwork reflecting traditional Damascene courtyards.

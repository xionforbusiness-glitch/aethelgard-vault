## 2. Alternative Comparison — De'Longhi Low-Pressure Gas Water Heater
- **Key Advantage:** Engineered specifically for **ultra-low water pressure** environments (gravity-fed rooftop tanks common in Damascus apartments where head pressure on upper floors is minimal).
- **Specifications & Considerations:**

  - Brass water valve assembly and copper heat exchanger options (verify pure copper vs. tinned exchanger weight at the vendor in Bab Musalla / Al-Manakhliyah).

## 3. Troubleshooting `"EE"` Error Code on Digital Gas Geysers
When a digital 10L gas water heater (Crystal / De'Longhi) displays **`EE`** on the LED screen and cuts off:
1. **Over-Temperature / Dry-Burn Protection ($>75^\circ\text{C}\text{–}85^\circ\text{C}$):**
   - **Cause:** Water flow is too low while gas flame is set too high (or set to "Winter" mode with restricted shower head flow), causing the high-limit thermal cutout switch (bimetallic thermostat on the heat exchanger pipe) to trip.
   - **Fix:** Increase water flow knob (turn toward *Max Water / Min Temp*), switch to *Summer* mode, clean shower head / inlet mesh filter, and allow unit to cool for 2 minutes.
2. **Flame Failure / Ionization Sensor Fault:**
   - **Cause:** Weak D-cell batteries, low LPG cylinder pressure / faulty gas regulator, or carbon soot on the flame ionization probe.
   - **Fix:** Replace both 1.5V D-cell Alkaline batteries, check LPG regulator flow, and gently clean the flame sensor rod with fine sandpaper if ignition clicks continuously before flashing `EE`.
3. **20-Minute Safety Timer Cutoff:**
   - Continuous operation beyond 20 minutes triggers automatic gas solenoid shutoff (`EE`) to prevent carbon monoxide buildup; simply close and reopen the hot water tap to reset.

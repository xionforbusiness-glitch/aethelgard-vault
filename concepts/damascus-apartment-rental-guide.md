## ⚡ 2. Critical Infrastructure Inspection Checklist

Before signing a lease on any unfurnished apartment in Damascus, verify these 5 points:

1. **Electricity & Alternative Power (الأمبيرات والطاقة الشمسية):**
   * Is the building connected to private neighborhood generator subscriptions (**خط أمبيرات**)?
   * If not, is the roof accessible to install a private solar inverter/battery setup?
2. **Water Supply & Pumping (المياه والمضخة):**
   * Check the building's water schedule (usually 2–3 days/week).
   * Ensure there is a dedicated roof tank (**خزان 1000–2000 لتر**) + functional electric water pump (**دينمو**).
3. **Floor Level & Elevators (الطابق والمصعد):**
   * 4th and 5th floors are significantly cheaper ($30–$50 less/mo). If there is no generator-backed elevator, confirm you are comfortable with the stairs.
4. **Sunlight & Ventilation (التهوية والشمس):**
   * Avoid deep basement floors (أقبية سفلية) due to humidity (رطوبة). Target 1st to 3rd floor for optimal light and insulation.
5. **Contract & Payment Terms (العقد والدفع):**
   * Standard payment terms: **6 months in advance (دفع سداسي)** or 1 year.
   * Real estate broker fee (**كومسيون المكتب**): Standard is 1 month's rent.
   * Municipal lease registration (**تثبيت العقد في البلدية / الإسكان**) is required for official residency and security clearance.

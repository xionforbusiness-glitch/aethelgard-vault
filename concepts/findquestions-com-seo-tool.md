# FindQuestions.com — SEO Customer Question Discovery Tool

A SaaS tool that scans Reddit to discover real customer questions related to specific business niches. Designed for SEO content creators, marketers, and business owners to find organic keyword opportunities.

**Website:** https://findquestions.com/  
**Creator:** HeyTony (Instagram/YouTube/Facebook)  
**Interface:** Dark-themed web application

---

## Value Proposition

> **"Find Questions Your Customers Are Asking"**

The tool extracts unfiltered, authentic questions potential customers ask on Reddit, giving businesses direct insight into customer pain points, confusion, and buying signals for SEO content strategy.

---

## How It Works

1. **Enter Niche:** User inputs what they do or sell (e.g., *dog groomer*, *roof repair*)
2. **Scanning:** Tool scans Reddit for related questions
3. **Preview Results:** Shows sample questions (typically 3–5)
4. **Email Gate:** Requires email for full 40-question PDF export

### Best Practices

> *Be specific! "roof repair" works better than "roofer"*

Specificity yields higher-quality, targeted questions.

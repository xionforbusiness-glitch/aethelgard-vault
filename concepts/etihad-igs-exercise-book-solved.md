# 📝 Etihad Airways IGS Exercise Book (100% Solved & Verified)

*Master revision question bank and verified exam solutions for the **Introduction to Ground Services (IGS)** curriculum.*

---

## 📋 Exercise 1: Foundation Knowledge & Core Rules

### 1. Name the four cabin services offered by Etihad:
* **a.** **The Residence** *(A380 ultra-luxury 3-room suite)*
* **b.** **First Class** *(First Suite / First Apartment)*
* **c.** **Business Class** *(Business Studio)*
* **d.** **Economy Class** *(including Economy Space)*

### 2. A guest is enquiring if the request for a Vegetarian Oriental Meal has been confirmed. What is the SSR code for the meal type requested?
* **Answer:** **`VOML`** *(Vegetarian Oriental Meal — prepared in Chinese/Oriental style without meat, fish, eggs, or dairy).*

### 3. Special meal requests must be made at least ______ hours prior to departure.
* **Answer:** **`24` hours** *(Must be booked at least 24 hours prior to scheduled departure time in the PNR).*

### 4. A guest requests a wheelchair at check-in and advises that they cannot climb steps. Which SSR code should be added?
* **Answer:** **`WCHS`** *(Wheelchair Steps — Guest cannot ascend/descend stairs, but can walk inside the aircraft cabin to their seat. Requires ambulift or ramp stair assistance).*

### 5. What is the cabin baggage (hand baggage) allowance for Economy?
* **Answer:** **`1 bag up to 7 kg`** *(Maximum dimensions 56 x 36 x 23 cm) + 1 personal item up to 5 kg (laptop bag, handbag, or duty-free pouch max 40 x 30 x 15 cm).*

### 6. Information on Etihad’s policies for guests requiring special assistance is contained in which manual?
* **Answer:** **`Guest Services Manual (GSM)`** *(Specifically Chapter 6: Special Categories of Passengers).*

### 7. What are the three mandatory security questions asked at check-in?
* **a.** **"Did you pack your bags yourself?"** *(and have they been in your possession at all times since packing?)*
* **b.** **"Are you carrying any items, gifts, or packages for anyone else?"**
* **c.** **"Are you carrying any hazardous or prohibited materials?"** *(e.g., spare lithium batteries, power banks, or e-cigarettes in checked bags, flammable liquids).*

### 8. Duty free is calculated as part of the total cabin baggage allowance.
* **Answer:** **`b. False`** *(Duty-free purchases in reasonable quantities inside approved sealed security bags (STEBs) are permitted in addition to standard cabin allowance).*

### 9. What is the cabin baggage (Hand Baggage) allowance for an infant?
* **Answer:** **`1 bag up to 5 kg`** *(Plus one fully collapsible stroller/pushchair or carrycot free of charge).*

### 10. Checked baggage exceeding _____kg cannot be accepted for carriage.
* **Answer:** **`32 kg`** *(70 lbs — Absolute single-piece occupational health and safety weight cap across the global network).*

### 11. A heavy tag must be placed on checked baggage weighing over _____kg.
* **Answer:** **`23 kg`** *(50 lbs — Any piece from 23.1 kg up to 32 kg requires a visible fluorescent "HEAVY" baggage tag).*

### 12. All fragile and damaged items accepted as baggage should be issued with a limited release tag (LRT).
* **Answer:** **`a. True`** *(Protects the airline from pre-existing damage claims; guest signs the LRT waiver).*

### 13. A guest checked-in a television exceeding the standard checked baggage dimensions and baggage allowance must pay for excess baggage in addition to the oversized baggage fee.
* **Answer:** **`a. True`** *(Both overweight/piece excess charges AND the `OSZF` oversize handling fee apply).*

### 14. Golf bags are exempt from the oversized baggage fee.
* **Answer:** **`a. True`** *(Etihad offers 1 complimentary golf set up to 15 kg per passenger free of charge and exempt from oversize fees).*

### 15. A guest is travelling with an extra seat for a musical instrument (CBBG). On what type of seat (narrow body aircraft) must the instrument be placed?
* **Answer:** **`a. Window Seat`** *(Must always be placed on a window seat to ensure it does not block the egress or aisle path during an evacuation).*

### 16. How many hours before departure can a guest check-in online? (web/mobile/Etihad app)
* **Answer:** **`30 hours`** *(Online check-in is open from 30 hours up to 1 hour prior to scheduled departure).*

### 17. Select the cabin baggage allowance for guest travelling in First and Business cabin to/from USA:
* **Answer:** **`c) 2 bags with combined weight not exceeding 12Kg + Personal item`** *(up to 5 kg).*

### 18. State True or False – Pooling of baggage is not permitted for guest traveling to the USA via Abu Dhabi.
* **Answer:** **`a. True`** *(US CBP Preclearance and TSA strict security regulations require individual baggage reconciliation for each passenger).*

### 19. State True or False – Staff travel bookings can be merged with commercial bookings if they belong to the same family.
* **Answer:** **`b. False`** *(Staff travel tickets are sub-load/standby or concession fares and cannot be merged into commercial PNRs).*

### 20. Select the 3 scenarios from below, when are you allowed to through tag bags for separate PNR’s provided there is a baggage agreement:
* **Answers:**
  * **`a) Two separate PNRs on EY operated flights with a minimum connection time of 60min`**
  * **`c) All confirmed staff travel tickets`**
  * **`d) Marine PNRs that contain the SEMN SSR`**

## 📋 Exercise 2: Check-in Rules, Seating & Dangerous Goods

### 1. A priority baggage label should be affixed to a guest travelling in Economy holding an Etihad Guest Silver card.
* **Answer:** **`b. False`** *(Priority baggage tags are reserved for First, Business, Etihad Guest Gold, and Etihad Guest Platinum members).*

### 2. A guest wants to carry an e-cigarette in their checked baggage, is it allowed?
* **Answer:** **`No`** *(E-cigarettes and vape devices must be carried on your person or in cabin baggage ONLY; strictly prohibited in checked baggage).*

### 3. Can we accept a smart bag with a removable lithium battery of 85Wh?
* **Answer:** **`Yes`** *(Smart bags are accepted if the battery is removable and under 100Wh. If the bag is checked into the hold, the battery must be removed and carried in the cabin).*

### 4. A bag accepted in a damaged condition must be affixed with a ________________ tag.
* **Answer:** **`Limited Release Tag (LRT)`**.

### 5. For guest travelling as a group to/from the USA, are you permitted to tag all baggage under 1 guest?
* **Answer:** **`No`** *(Strictly prohibited for US-bound flights due to US CBP security protocols).*

### 6. Etihad guest GOLD members can pre-reserve a seat in Economy free of charge.
* **Answer:** **`Yes`** *(Gold and Platinum members receive complimentary standard and forward seat selection).*

### 7. Can a guest purchase an extra seat (EXST) at the check-in counter?
* **Answer:** **`No`** *(Extra seats require advance reservation and ticketing through reservations/call center to ensure aircraft weight and balance manifest integration).*

### 8. A guest can pay to upgrade (Instant upgrade) at check-in from Economy to First subject to seat and catering availability.
* **Answer:** **`b. False`** *(Airport Instant Upgrades are single-cabin upgrades: Economy → Business, or Business → First. Direct jump from Economy to First is not permitted via instant upgrade).*

### 9. Can a 16-year-old be assigned a seat on the emergency exit row?
* **Answer:** **`Yes`** *(Minimum age for emergency exit row seating on Etihad is 15 years old, provided they are able-bodied and willing to assist in an emergency).*

### 10. What is the minimum age at which an infant can travel on Etihad?
* **Answer:** **`7 days`** *(Infants under 7 days of age require an approved Etihad Medical Certificate / MEDIF).*

### 11. What does EMD stand for?
* **Answer:** **`Electronic Miscellaneous Document`** *(Standard IATA digital voucher for excess baggage, seat purchases, upgrades, and lounge access).*

### 12. What is the maximum weight of an infant to use the bassinet on board?
* **Answer:** **`11 kg`** *(And up to 75 cm / 29.5 inches in length).*

### 13. A portable car seat can be accepted for use on board provided the child is between ___ and ___ months and it is suitable for aircraft use.
* **Answer:** **`6` and `36` months** *(Must have approved aviation authority certification stickers like FAA/CAA/ECE).*

### 14. A guest travelling alone with 2 infants (twins) can be accepted for travel.
* **Answer:** **`Yes`** *(Accepted if one infant sits on the adult's lap and the second infant occupies a separate purchased seat in an approved car seat).*

### 15. Can a 15-year-old child travel alone? Can a 10-year-old child travel alone?
* **Answer:**
  * **15-year-old:** **`Yes`** *(Classified as a Young Passenger Travelling Alone / `YPTA` — optional UMNR service).*
  * **10-year-old:** **`Yes`** *(Classified as an Unaccompanied Minor / `UMNR` — mandatory UMNR supervision service).*

### 16. Guests requiring a stretcher (STCR) need an Etihad Medical Certificate to travel.
* **Answer:** **`Yes`** *(Mandatory MEDIF clearance from Etihad Aviation Medical Services).*

### 17. What does UMNR stand for? UMs are considered between the age of _______and _______ years.
* **Answer:** **`Unaccompanied Minor`**; between **`5` and `11` years** *(inclusive).*

### 18. Young passengers (YPTA) are considered between _____and _____years.
* **Answer:** **`12` and `17` years**.

### 19. Select the 6 sporting equipment that are exempted from oversized baggage fees:
* **Answers:**
  * **`a) Ski equipment`**
  * **`b) Scuba diving equipment`**
  * **`c) Water ski equipment`**
  * **`d) Fishing equipment`**
  * **`e) Cricket equipment`**
  * **`g) Golf`** *(1 set up to 15 kg free)*

### 20. When would you charge for oversized baggage fees for television?
* **Answer:** **`c) From 40 inches`** *(Televisions 40 inches and above incur the `OSZF` oversize handling fee).*

### 21. Pets (PETC) are permitted in First Class with prior approval from Flight Operations only.
* **Answer:** **`No`** *(Pets in cabin are accepted in Business and Economy cabins; not permitted in First Class suites / Residence due to cabin configuration and safety).*

### 22. Guest travelling with PETC can be assigned an exit seat.
* **Answer:** **`b. False`** *(PETC passengers can never sit in exit rows or bulkhead rows; pet carrier must stow under the seat in front).*

### 23. Can we assign a bulkhead seat for a guest travelling with an infant and a bassinet (BSCT) request, free of charge?
* **Answer:** **`Yes`** *(Bassinet positions are fixed to bulkhead walls and assigned complimentary to confirmed BSCT guests).*

### 24. Select the 4 benefits that are associated with the purchase of Airport Instant Upgrade:
* **Answers:**
  * **`a) Cabin baggage as per new class of travel`**
  * **`b) Lounge access as per new class of travel`**
  * **`d) Priority baggage tagging`**
  * **`e) Priority boarding`**
  *(Note: Chauffeur LIMO is not included; original ticket checked baggage allowance applies).*

### 25. What is the service code added for Etihad Select Upgrade (Plusgrade/online bidding) after it is processed successfully?
* **Answer:** **`c) UPGP`**.

### 26. Operational upgrades do not include additional entitlements such as lounge access and extra baggage allowances.
* **Answer:** **`a. True`** *(Operational/involuntary upgrades provide only the upgraded seat and in-flight catering).*

### 27. What are the three benefits of purchasing priority (PRIO) access?
* **1.** **Priority Check-in** *(Dedicated Business/Priority counter)*
* **2.** **Priority Boarding** *(Fast-track gate lane)*
* **3.** **Priority Baggage Delivery** *(Priority tags for early carousel delivery)*

### 28. What is the maximum number of unaccompanied minors (UMNR) that can be accepted on Etihad flights?
* **Answer:** **`d) No limit`** *(Subject to cabin crew ratio and station acceptance policy).*

### 29. What is the minimum age for acceptance of accompanied minors travelling in different cabins?
* **Answer:** **`b) 5 years`** *(Children under 5 must sit in the same cabin as the accompanying adult).*

## 📋 Exercise 3: Medical Clearance, PRM & Passenger Handling

### 1. Expectant mothers require a medical certificate from the beginning of the ______week of pregnancy.
* **Answer:** **`29th` week** *(Weeks 1–28 require no certificate; weeks 29–36 for single pregnancy require MEDIF/medical certificate).*

### 2. Can we accept a medical certificate in French, if verified by an Etihad Staff/ Representative?
* **Answer:** **`No`** *(Must be written in English or Arabic and issued by a certified physician).*

### 3. When would you use the following SSR codes:
* **a. WCHR:** **Wheelchair Ramp** — Guest can walk up/down steps and make their own way to seat, but requires wheelchair for long distances across the terminal.
* **b. WCHS:** **Wheelchair Steps** — Guest cannot ascend/descend aircraft steps, but can walk inside cabin to their seat. Requires ambulift or ramp stair assistance.
* **c. WCHC:** **Wheelchair Cabin** — Guest is completely immobile and requires wheelchair assistance all the way to their seat inside the cabin. Never seat in Emergency Exit rows.

### 4. Mobility aids (wheelchairs, crutches, etc.) may be carried free of charge, in addition to the ticketed baggage allowance.
* **Answer:** **`Yes`** *(Up to 2 mobility devices per guest free of charge).*

### 5. Can a ground staff source a volunteer to act as a safety assistant?
* **Answer:** **`No`** *(Safety assistants must be designated by the passenger or pre-arranged before travel; staff cannot recruit random passengers).*

### 6. A guest wishing to use their own portable oxygen concentrator in flight requires prior medical clearance.
* **Answer:** **`a. True`** *(Must be on the approved FAA/Etihad POC device list with battery capacity covering 150% of flight duration).*

### 7. If in doubt of a guest’s fitness to fly, what should be completed?
* **Answer:** **`MEDIF (Medical Information Form) / Passenger Medical Clearance`** *(Contact Aviation Medical Services via Hub Duty Manager).*

### 8. A continuous positive airway pressure (CPAP) device requires medical clearance.
* **Answer:** **`b. False`** *(CPAP / BiPAP does not require formal MEDIF medical clearance, provided it is an approved model running on dry/lithium battery).*

### 9. An unaccompanied minor service fee is applicable for any request for assistance for children aged 5 - 17 years, at the airport (not booked in advance).
* **Answer:** **`a. True`** *(Late airport UMNR booking fee applies).*

### 10. Tracksuits and torn jeans are acceptable for a staff traveler in the Business cabin.
* **Answer:** **`b. False`** *(Strict smart-casual grooming and dress code standards apply for staff concession travel).*

### 11. Can a meal waiver form be offered to commercial guests as a last resort?
* **Answer:** **`Yes`** *(Offered when a requested special meal could not be catered, allowing the guest to choose standard catering if safe).*

### 12. Jump seats may be assigned to Etihad staff based in Head Quarters in Abu Dhabi.
* **Answer:** **`Yes`** *(Subject to Captain authority, GCAA approval, and operating staff eligibility).*

### 13. Name the tiers of the Etihad Guest (Frequent Traveler Programme):
* **a.** **Bronze** *(Entry tier)*
* **b.** **Silver**
* **c.** **Gold**
* **d.** **Platinum**
* **e.** **Exclusive** *(VIP invitation-only tier)*

### 14. Can an Etihad Guest Silver cardholder travelling in Economy, access the Etihad Lounge in Abu Dhabi?
* **Answer:** **`No`** *(Silver members receive complimentary access to Al Reem Lounge / paid access; Etihad First/Business Lounge requires Gold or Platinum).*

### 15. Expectant mothers expecting a single child can travel with a valid medical certificate up to the end of ______ weeks.
* **Answer:** **`c) 36 weeks`** *(Multiple pregnancy e.g. twins permitted up to end of 32 weeks).*

### 16. What is the validity of medical certificate issued for expectant mothers?
* **Answer:** **`b) 14 days`** *(Must be issued within 14 days of travel date).*

## 📋 Exercise 4: Baggage Allowances, Security & Special Ops

### 1. What is the additional baggage allowance for a member of the Government Travel Program - Ministry of Foreign Affairs (MoFA), except nil baggage fare?
* **Weight concept:** **`+10 kg`**
* **Piece Concept:** **`+1 piece (23 kg)`**

### 2. Guests operationally upgraded (free upgrade) from Economy to Business are entitled to lounge access.
* **Answer:** **`b. False`** *(Operational upgrades do not include lounge access or increased baggage allowance).*

### 3. Service animals (dogs) can be accepted for carriage in the cabin.
* **Answer:** **`a. True`** *(Guide dogs / service dogs `SVAN` travel free of charge in cabin on all routes).*

### 4. Hunting rifles require prior approval to be accepted as checked baggage.
* **Answer:** **`a. True`** *(Requires `WEAP` SSR, security clearances, and police authorization).*

### 5. What is the maximum amount of ammunition which can be checked in per guest?
* **Answer:** **`5 kg`** *(Gross weight per passenger, Div 1.4S, securely boxed, for sporting/hunting purposes).*

### 6. Only cats and dogs are allowed to be carried as pets in cabin, with prior booking/clearance.
* **Answer:** **`b. False`** *(Falcons are also officially permitted in the cabin on Etihad Airways).*

### 7. Which department in AUH should be contacted to provide compensation in case of Denied Boarding?
* **Answer:** **`Guest Service Centre (GSC) / Hub Operations Control (HOC)`**.

### 8. Hoverboards are allowed to be carried in checked-in baggage only.
* **Answer:** **`b. False`** *(Hoverboards and self-balancing lithium vehicles are strictly prohibited everywhere on the aircraft — neither cabin nor checked).*

### 9. When is the MUCF SSR used?
* **Answer:** **`Manual Upgrade from Business to First`**.

### 10. Guests travelling on pre-cleared direct flights to the USA with Etihad Airways will be processed through the US CBP Pre-clearance facility at AUH Airport (when it is open).
* **Answer:** **`a. True`** *(Passengers clear US immigration and customs in Abu Dhabi and arrive as domestic passengers in the US).*

### 11. Etihad Duty Supervisors can authorize carriage of drones powered by lithium batteries when the Watt hour is above 100 but less than 160.
* **Answer:** **`a. True`** *(Between 100Wh and 160Wh requires airline supervisor approval).*

### 12. A standby Tag should be used on bags of guests with onward space available (SA) connections, regardless of whether the guest has been allocated a seat for the onward sector.
* **Answer:** **`a. True`**.

### 13. Cremated ashes in Urns can be carried as:
* **Answers:** **`a) Cabin baggage`** and **`b) Checked baggage`** *(Must have cremation certificate and sealed lead-free container).*

### 14. Which of the 3 below medical equipment requires a valid Etihad medical certificate?
* **Answers:**
  * **`c) Respirators`**
  * **`d) Ventilators`**
  * **`e) Portable Oxygen Concentrator (POC)`** *(requiring in-flight battery power and medical clearance)*

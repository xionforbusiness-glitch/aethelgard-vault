## 🧰 2. Essential Handouts Cheat Sheet: Master Before Monday

### A. Core IATA Special Service Request (SSR) Codes

In Etihad's Amadeus Altéa DCS, these codes dictate specific handling rules, seating restrictions, and airport ramp escorts:

| SSR Code | Full Meaning | Operational Ground Handling Rules & Restrictions |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **`WCHR`** | Wheelchair (Ramp) | Guest can ascend/descend steps and walk to seat; requires wheelchair only across terminal/ramp. |
| **`WCHS`** | Wheelchair (Steps) | Guest cannot ascend/descend steps, but can walk inside cabin to seat; requires ambulift / ramp lift. |
| **`WCHC`** | Wheelchair (Cabin) | Guest completely immobile; requires aisle wheelchair and manual lift to seat. **Never seat in Emergency Exit row.** |
| **`UMNR`** | Unaccompanied Minor | Children traveling alone (ages 5–11). Mandatory **UMNR pouch**, passport handover, and direct gate-to-cabin escort. |
| **`MEDA`** | Medical Case | Requires airline medical clearance (MEDIF form) and oxygen/stretcher equipment coordination. |
| **`BLND` / `DEAF`** | Blind / Deaf Guest | Requires individual safety briefing and priority boarding escort. |
| **`PETC`** | Pet in Cabin | Small pet/falcon inside approved carrier under seat (max weight limits apply). |
| **`AVIH`** | Animal in Hold | Live animal transported in pressurized, temperature-controlled cargo hold. |
| **`INAD`** | Inadmissible Passenger | Passenger refused entry by immigration at transit/destination; handled under strict custody. |
| **`DEPA` / `DEPU`**| Deportee (Accompanied/Unaccompanied) | Escorted removal passenger; captain must be notified prior to boarding. |
| **`VIP` / `CIP`** | Very Important Person / Commercially Important | Executive greeting, fast-track immigration, and premium lounge escort. |

## 🌟 3. Etihad Visual & Service Standards Guide (The "Etihad Touch")

1. **Guest Nomenclature:** Never use the word "passenger" or "pax" when communicating with customers. Always address travelers as **"Guest" (ضيف)**.
2. **Grooming Excellence:** Spotless uniform pressing, polished black shoes, groomed hair, and official Etihad name tag aligned on the left chest.
3. **Counter Setup:** Clean check-in desks, zero clutter, stock of First/Business/Priority baggage tags, boarding pass cardstock pre-loaded, and baggage scales calibrated.
4. **Active Welcoming:** Greet approaching guests with eye contact, a warm smile, and the signature greeting: *"Welcome to Etihad Airways, Mr./Ms. [Last Name]."*

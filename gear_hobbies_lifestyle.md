### Hardware Collection
- **MoYu RS3M MagLev:** Primary competition cube; features magnetic levitation repulsion instead of traditional springs for friction reduction.
- **QiYi Warrior S:** Reliable secondary/backup tactile training cube.

### Algorithm Training
- **Current Objective:** Full single-look OLL (Orientation of the Last Layer).
- **Notable Case — OLL 23 (Headlights):**
  - **Formula:**
    $$R2 \cdot D \cdot (R' \cdot U2 \cdot R) \cdot D' \cdot (R' \cdot U2 \cdot R')$$
  - **Recognition:** All corners misoriented with two headlights facing front/back.

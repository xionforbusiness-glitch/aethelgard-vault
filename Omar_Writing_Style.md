- **Tone:** Direct, confident, technically detailed, professional without corporate fluff.
- **Average Body Length:** ~112 words.
- **Greeting Style:** `Short and direct: 'Hi [Name],' or 'Dear [Team/Hiring Manager],'`

- DO NOT use excessive exclamation points or hyperbolic self-praise.

# 🍙 Komemaru (مطعم كوميمارو — こめ丸) — Japanese Street Kitchen

![[komemaru_japanese_restaurant_damascus.jpg]]

**Komemaru (こめ丸)** is the first dedicated authentic Japanese street food and *Yatai/Izakaya* kitchen concept established in Damascus, Syria. It combines a vibrant Neo-Tokyo cyberpunk neon aesthetic with traditional Japanese comfort dishes, street-food platters, ramen, and sushi.

---

## 📍 Location & Directions

* **Country & City:** Syria (🇸🇾), Damascus (دمشق)
* **Neighborhood / District:** **Mashrou' Dummar (مشروع دمر)**
* **Vibe / Setup:** Japanese street kiosk / *Yatai (屋台)* counter with open kitchen, hanging traditional red paper lanterns (*Chōchin*), and an illuminated Tokyo neon art wall.
* **Directions Hint:** Located within the central commercial market / dining strip of **Mashrou' Dummar** (مشروع دمر — دمشق).

---

## 🍱 Menu Highlights & Signature Dishes

| Category | Dish / Item | Description & Taste Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Specialty Boxes** | **Tokyo Drift (طوكيو دريفت)** | Premium Japanese street box featuring curated sushi/maki rolls accompanied by authentic Japanese wasabi and signature dipping sauces. |
| **Cooked & Crispy** | **Karaage Chicken (دجاج كاراغي)** | Traditional Japanese sesame-crusted fried chicken bites — highly recommended for diners who prefer fully cooked dishes over raw sushi. |
| **Sides & Fries** | **Komemaru Seasoned Fries** | Fries tossed in proprietary Japanese nori/umami seasoning blends with custom mayonnaise and dipping glazes. |
| **Noodles & Mains** | **Matcha Noodles & Ramen** | Handcrafted noodles infused with Japanese green tea / matcha broth bases, classic ramen bowls with ajitsuke tamago eggs. |
| **Seafood & Street Fare**| **Japanese Shrimp & Salads** | Crispy shrimp platters and fresh seaweed/Asian fusion salads. |
| **Japanese Sweets** | **Mochi (أوموتشي — おもち)** | Traditional chewy Japanese glutinous rice cakes with sweet fillings. |

---

## 💡 Practical Recommendations
1. **For First-Timers:** If hesitant about raw sashimi/fish, start with the **Karaage Chicken (كاراغي)** and **Matcha Noodles**.
2. **For Sushi Lovers:** Order the **Tokyo Drift Box** with extra wasabi.
3. **Atmosphere:** Great spot for evening street-food vibes and aesthetic photos with the Neo-Tokyo neon sign wall.

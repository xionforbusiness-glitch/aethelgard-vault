# ✈️ Etihad Airways (الاتحاد للطيران — EY / ETD) Station & Ground Operations Playbook

**Etihad Airways** (`IATA: EY | ICAO: ETD | Callcode: ETTHAD`) is the national flag carrier of the United Arab Emirates (UAE), headquartered in Khalifa City, Abu Dhabi, with its primary super-hub at **Zayed International Airport (AUH)**.

Alongside Emirates (`EK`) and Qatar Airways (`QR`), Etihad forms the prestigious **"ME3" (Middle East Big Three)** global super-connector airlines.

## 💼 2. What Handling Etihad Means for an Airport Check-in Agent

Working on the **Etihad Airways station counter** is one of the highest-reputation credentials you can have on your aviation resume:

### 1. Ultra-High Service & Grooming Standards
* **The "Etihad Standard":** Impeccable uniform grooming, structured VIP greeting protocols, and active hospitality.
* **Premium Cabin Tiers:** *The Residence* (A380), *First Class*, *Business Studio*, and *Economy Space*.

### 2. Global Transit Complexity (The Abu Dhabi Hub Network)
* Unlike point-to-point flights where passengers only fly to one destination, **80%+ of Etihad passengers are transit travelers** connecting in Abu Dhabi (`AUH`) onwards to:
  * North America (New York `JFK`, Chicago `ORD`, Washington `IAD`, Toronto `YYZ`)
  * Europe (London `LHR`, Paris `CDG`, Frankfurt `FRA`)
  * Asia & Australia (Sydney `SYD`, Melbourne `MEL`, Tokyo `NRT`, Bangkok `BKK`)
* **Critical Ground Duty:** Verifying **TIMATIC** immigration rules, transit visas, return tickets, and onward boarding passes for complex multi-leg journeys.

### 3. US Preclearance Awareness
* Abu Dhabi (`AUH`) hosts a dedicated **US Customs and Border Protection (CBP) Preclearance** facility. Passengers bound for the USA clear US immigration in Abu Dhabi before departure. Ground handling agents at origin stations must ensure flawless US APIS (Advance Passenger Information System) data entry.

## 🧰 3. Departure Control System (DCS) & Baggage Protocols

### Departure Control: Amadeus Altéa DCS
* Etihad utilizes **Amadeus Altéa Customer Management (CM)** for check-in, seat maps, e-ticket coupon synchronization, and standby lists.
* If you have experience on Amadeus or global DCS, Altéa is the worldwide industry gold standard.

### Baggage Allowance & Tagging
* **Piece Concept vs Weight Concept:** Strictly enforced based on route (Piece concept for US/Canada, Weight concept for Middle East/Asia).
* **Priority Tagging:** 
  * 🟡 **Yellow First Class:** First priority offload at AUH.
  * 🔴 **Red Business Class:** Dedicated baggage priority delivery.
  * 🔵 **Etihad Guest Tiers:** Platinum, Gold, Silver tier allowances.

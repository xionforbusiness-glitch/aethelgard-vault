## 📌 Summary & Content
phone-harness: Let AI Drive Your Phone

phone-harness is the developer tool that finally lets your coding agent operate a real physical phone. It solves the problem of digital isolation by giving artificial intelligence direct control over your device, allowing it to scroll, tap, and type exactly as you would. The project works with both iPhones and Androids, using standard operating system features rather than requiring you to jailbreak or install risky third-party software. This is particularly fascinating because it turns your personal device into a tool that your software can command, enabling complex tasks like booking rides or reading notifications automatically. It bridges the gap between code and the physical world with surprising simplicity.

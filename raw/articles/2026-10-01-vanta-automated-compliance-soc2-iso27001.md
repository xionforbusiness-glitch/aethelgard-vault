## Executive Summary
Vanta is a cloud-based security compliance and trust management platform founded by Christina Cacioppo in 2018. It automates up to 85–90% of the manual evidence-gathering and policy-drafting processes required to achieve enterprise security certifications (SOC 2 Type I/II, ISO 27001, HIPAA, GDPR, NIST CSF, PCI DSS).
Instead of passing static annual spreadsheets and manual screenshots to auditors, Vanta connects via read-only APIs and agent telemetry to cloud providers (AWS, GCP, Azure), identity providers (Okta, Google Workspace), code repositories (GitHub, GitLab), and HR systems to continuously monitor security controls in real time.
